Native-asset authorization protocol
9 November 2026

The selected profile authorizes a canonical request by knowledge of a
Parano1d-derived authority secret. EVM and Solana vaults protect their
own native assets and verify the proof before releasing value.

Authority

HKDF-SHA256 separates the 256-bit Parano1d secret by network and account
index. Fourteen field elements fill the authority-secret positions of a
width-24 Poseidon2 permutation. Ten capacity elements are fixed by its
public authority-domain hash. The permutation uses eight full rounds,
26 partial rounds and a cubic S-box. Its first twelve outputs identify
the authority. The vault stores their 32-byte domain-separated commitment.

Canonical request

Both SHA256 and Keccak256 digest the same request encoding. It includes
the network, deployed vault or program, parameter manifest, nonce,
recipient, amount and expiry. Sixteen u16 limbs of the first digest enter
the AIR. The second digest enters the challenger's initial state.
The host recomputes both components from the transfer being requested.

Field and proof geometry

Base field:                   p=2130706433.
Extension:                    degree 8, X^8-3.
Logical trace:                128 rows, 40 columns.
Execution and rotation:       odd rows, stride 2, order-64 rotation.
Private state-mask dimension: 192 per column.
Preprocessing:                28 columns.
Committed degree bound:       256.
Codeword domain:              4096 positions.
FRI checks:                   64 distinct positions.
FRI arities:                  4,4,4,4.
Field salts:                  20 per matrix row.
Query grinding:               12 bits.
Hash:                         SHA256||Keccak256, exact 64-byte roots.

The 64 explicit free logical rows and 128 hiding-PCS rows supply the
state masks. Capacity is constrained at logical row one. The authority
output is constrained at row 127. Main transport needs at most 144
coordinates. Every quotient mask needs at most 72 of its 128 coordinates.

Transcript

Each extension challenge consumes a complete hybrid response and takes
its eight radix-p digits. The seven field challenges are AIR alpha,
out-of-domain z, PCS gamma and four FRI betas. Every next hash input
retains the complete preceding digest.

After the final polynomial, one seal digest binds the query phase.
A reduced field witness is appended to that digest. The next 512-bit
response has twelve zero low bits. Its remaining 500 bits are reduced
modulo C(4096,64) and unranked as one distinct 64-element subset.

The source acceptance bound uses at most 896 good source positions.
Its exact native query density is
ceil(2^500/C(4096,64))*C(896,64)/2^512. The finite field numerator and
source decoder are in research/security/rs-certificate.json.

EVM acceptance

The installed vault fixes its authority, manifest and verifier. Its
canonical request includes the current nonce. A job is indexed by the
SHA256 and Keccak256 pair on the complete base and request. Initialization
checks the AIR and prepares a cache authenticated by both components.
Consecutive batches contain at most twelve ordinary query witnesses.
After all 64 pass, the vault consumes the nonce and transfers value.
The complete selected lifecycle takes seven transactions.

Solana acceptance

The native vault is a PDA derived from the program and authority
commitment. A program-owned temporary account retains the canonical
request pair, base pair, uploader, query counter, rank counter and cache.
Uploads use generation-labelled, idempotent windows. The program rejects
conflicting duplicates and skips. A reset preserves accepted progress.

The base is 9332 bytes. It includes 268 extension evaluations, seven
proof roots, the final polynomial, twelve authority outputs, three
inverse hints, 64 u16 position values and the grinding witness.
The derived cache is 9858 bytes. An expanded query is 7520 bytes.

Queries use eight leaves and 76 full sibling digests. A twelve-byte
bitmap selects literal components. At a divergence, the omitted sibling
is derived from the previous verified leaf and its preceding path.
Every reconstructed query is checked by the ordinary verifier.

Each existing verification window accumulates its position rank terms
once. Exact final rank equality is checked before payout. Groups of six
queries give eleven query windows plus initial preparation. The selected
signed-v1 local lifecycle uses 91 transactions and peaks at 1,353,196 CU.
The temporary account reserve is refunded when the account closes.
