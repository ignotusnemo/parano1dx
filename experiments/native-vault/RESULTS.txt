Validation

The selected lifecycles verify all 64 positions, reject modified requests,
paths, field values and inverse hints, protect contiguous progress,
consume the nonce once and recover after an interrupted relayer.
The native streamed checker validates all query batch sizes one to six.
Signed Solana v1 wires are checked with their corresponding local SBF
instruction replay. These are local execution measurements.

Security

The exact quantum source density is about 2^-154.941. The complete ideal
resource envelope is below 0.325524 at the Category 1 reference budgets
under the explicit batch response-price condition c>=2^20. Its
primitive-advantage margin is 0.174476726375217. The security report contains the full native
reduction, finite calculations and exact application conditions.

The preliminary NIST-style resource assessment evaluates native
fixed-prefix SHA-256 and Keccak circuits, grind-output projections
and a one-bit phase construction. The smallest sampled data product
is 3,014,449 under optimistic unit-cost XOR/AND accounting. The report
states the evaluated circuit family and broader cryptanalytic conditions.
