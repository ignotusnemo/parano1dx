Authority primitives and fixed parameters
9 November 2026

The authorization reduction uses the exact fixed authority-preimage
game of authorization.txt, the joint HKDF game and the joint private
mask-and-salt generator game. Their advantages are separate terms in
the full resource corollary.

Poseidon2 uses KoalaBear p=2130706433, width 24, fourteen secret elements,
ten capacity elements and twelve output elements. The capacity is fixed
by H(AUTH_DOMAIN). The permutation has eight full rounds and 26 partial
rounds. The official Grain generator and round inequalities are
reproduced by poseidon_parameters.py. The reference table agrees in
all 215 canonical 8/23 constants before generation of the selected
8/26 constants. The selected round count satisfies the generator's
classical target-256 inequalities and its margin rules.

The concrete authority condition is preimage resistance for the full
installed commitment under the exact fixed constants, capacity and
secret distribution. Its resource budget includes the complete
extractor described in authorization.txt. The cryptanalysis, matrix
checks and round generator support this condition with reproducible
evidence. The Category 1 certificate records its required advantage
margin explicitly.

One Parano1d secret is 256 bits. HKDF-SHA256 separates networks and
account indices. Fourteen 32-bit words are reduced into F_p. The exact
residue distribution has either two or three word preimages per field
element. This distribution is preserved in the authority game.

Fresh 256-bit operating-system randomness seeds each proof. Distinct
SHA256-labelled branches feed the field-mask generator, the Merkle salt
generator and the free-row generator. Their joint output is replaced by
independent uniform field coins in the private-generator hybrid. The
simulator uses twenty independent field salts per matrix row in that
hybrid. Public preprocessing has its own deterministic seed.

check_profile.py proves primality by Proth's certificate, irreducibility
of X^8-3 by Rabin's criterion and bijectivity of the cube on F_p. It checks
both linear-layer ranks, 69 nonzero base-block minors, 96 internal-layer
observability powers, the order-64 rotation and all finite mask budgets.
check_mask_transport.py checks the exact production-size polynomial
translations, all eight quotient cosets and the full aggregate identity.
check_rs_certificate.py checks five finite interpolation certificates
and thirty native Fourier-fold basis identities.
