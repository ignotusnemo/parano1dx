Algebraic simulation for the fixed native-vault profile
9 November 2026

The polynomial opening view admits an efficient witness-free simulator
outside an explicit set of exceptional verifier coins. Its proof uses
affine translations of the actual main, quotient and randomization masks.

1. Model and notation

Let F = F_p, p = 2130706433, and E/F have degree eight. Private field
draws are independent and uniform in this section. Verifier challenges
are fresh uniform extension-field elements. Commitment digests and
authentication paths belong to the native hash compiler in compiler.txt.

The logical AIR has 128 rows and transition stride two. Its execution
rows are 1,3,...,127. In the committed order-256 domain these are the
coset where X^64=-1. Let V(X)=X^64+1. The remaining 192 values per
private state column are independent uniform masks: 64 explicit free
logical rows and 128 hiding-PCS rows. Therefore

    T_j(X)=U_j(X)+V(X)r_j(X), deg U_j<64, deg r_j<192.

This follows from the evaluation isomorphism on 256 distinct points.
The request columns encode public context and can be coupled identically.
The execution rotation g has order 64. Quotient chunks have 128 fresh
mask coordinates and committed degree below 256. Randomization columns
have degree below 256. The common code domain still has 4096 points.
The AIR divisor is Z_H=X^128-1. Its actual padded active selector is
A=(1-X^64+X^128-X^192)/4. Hence A*V=-(X^256-1)/4, a multiple
of Z_H. Every execution selector vanishes on the free logical rows. Capacity is constrained at row one. Last-row output
constraints apply at row 127.

Use alpha for the AIR-composition challenge, z for the out-of-domain
point, gamma for the PCS batching challenge and beta_i for FRI folding
challenges. Let Q be the set of distinct queried source points. There are
64 query positions, so |Q| <= 64. The proof opens main columns at z and
gz, quotient columns at z and randomization columns at z.

The opening view consists of all disclosed base and extension values,
the final FRI polynomial and every disclosed FRI row. In particular a
FRI row of arity four contains four aggregate values. Each input batch
opens one source row at each queried source location. Uniform simulation
of the complete aggregate polynomial handles all four FRI row values.

2. Interpolation and Frobenius orbits

For a in E, let O(a) be its Frobenius orbit over F. Its cardinality is
the degree of its minimal polynomial m_a. Values of an F-polynomial at
O(a) must be Frobenius conjugates. Conversely, arbitrary compatible values
at distinct orbits and base-field points interpolate to an F-polynomial
whose degree is below the sum of the orbit degrees. This follows from
the Chinese remainder theorem for their pairwise coprime minimal
polynomials, or equivalently from interpolation over the splitting field.

If a != 0, then O(a) and O(ga) are disjoint. Otherwise a^(p^j)=ga for
some 1 <= j < 8. Iterating the j-th Frobenius 8/gcd(j,8) times gives
g^(8/gcd(j,8))=1. This contradicts the order 64 of g. For a=0 the two
points coincide and impose the same evaluation condition.

3. Main-column transport

Take two valid witnesses for the same public (Y,I). Fix alpha, z, gamma,
the FRI challenges and Q. Exclude z in the public trace or code domains
where the following evaluation denominators can vanish. These public
domains and their g-rotations contain at most 8192 points.

Couple the two masked main traces to agree on

    Q union gQ union O(z) union O(gz).

This requires at most 2*64+2*8=144 base-field coordinates. At these
points V is nonzero. Interpolate the required change in r_j after
division by V. It has degree below 144, hence below 192. Adding this
fixed interpolant is a bijection of the uniform main-mask space.

All main observations are equal under this coupling. Both local and
rotated trace values needed to evaluate constraints agree at Q and O(z).
The implementation's conservative runtime budget is

    64 + 8*2 = 80 <= 128 for direct PCS disclosures.

The state transport uses 144 <= 192 coordinates. Each quotient mask
uses 72 <= 128. These separate inequalities justify both the runtime
PCS guard and the additional AIR transport.

The request columns are public and identical between the two witnesses.

4. Quotient transport

Each individual valid AIR-constraint polynomial C_l(X) is divisible by
Z_H. Expand the combined quotient in a fixed F-basis e_b of E:

    sum_l alpha^l C_l(X)/Z_H(X) = sum_b e_b Q_b(X).

Its basis coefficients are F-polynomials. The transported main values
imply that each Q_b agrees between the two witnesses at Q union O(z).
This conclusion follows by expanding every alpha^l in the fixed basis
before evaluation. It treats all disclosed quotient-basis openings.

There are eight disjoint quotient cosets H_i of size 128. Write V_i for
their monic vanishing polynomials and

    L_i = c_i product_(j != i) V_j.

The unmasked Lagrange chunks q_i satisfy sum_i L_i q_i = Q_b. Their
masks are

    q'_i = q_i + V_i t_i,                 0 <= i < 7,
    q'_7 = q_7 - V_7 sum_(i<7) (c_i/c_7)t_i,

with independent uniform F-polynomials t_i of degree below 128. The added
terms cancel in the weighted sum.

At each observation point outside all H_i, choose changes in the first
seven t_i to cancel the first seven chunk differences. The equality of
the combined Q_b forces cancellation in the last chunk. At a point of
H_i the corresponding chunk already agrees because its selector is one.
The other seven chunk values are adjustable. If i<7, its unused t_i
value supplies the remaining last-chunk adjustment. If i=7, the last
mask vanishes and the other seven adjustments are free.

These adjustments are Frobenius compatible. Interpolating them requires
at most |Q|+8 <= 72 coordinates per independent mask. Their degrees
remain below 128. Conditional on the main masks, each adjustment is a
translation of the uniform quotient-mask space. It preserves the full
joint distribution, including the balancing relation for chunk seven.
Extra random padding columns can be coupled identically.

5. Complete FRI aggregate transport

Let W be the initial DEEP batched polynomial. A source column P opened
at a contributes a known power of gamma times

    (P(X)-P(a))/(X-a).

All source columns have degree below 256, so deg W < 255. Couple the
randomizer's disclosed observations identically at first. Let Delta W
be the remaining aggregate difference from the transported main and
quotient polynomials.

The coupled direct observations make Delta W vanish at Q. It also
vanishes at every point in O(z) except z. To see this, evaluate each
source difference separately. Its numerator vanishes at the entire
relevant orbit. For a=z it still has the factor m_z(X)/(X-z). For the
main opening a=gz, orbit disjointness supplies this same factor. The case
z=0 has no additional orbit point to check.

Consequently

    Delta W(X) = A(X) B(X),
    A(X) = Z_Q(X) m_z(X)/(X-z),
    deg B < 256-|Q|-deg(m_z),

where Z_Q is the monic product of X-q over distinct q in Q.

The randomization batch contains sixteen independent F-polynomials of
degree below 256. The first eight are batched with powers
1,gamma,...,gamma^7. If gamma has degree eight over F, these powers form
an F-basis of E. Decompose

    -B(X) = sum_(j=0)^7 gamma^j B_j(X),   B_j in F[X].

Translate these eight randomization columns by

    R_j(X) <- R_j(X) + Z_Q(X)m_z(X) B_j(X).

The degree remains below 256. Every randomization observation at Q and
z is preserved. The aggregate change is -Delta W, because the DEEP
denominator is X-z. Hence the complete initial aggregate W, all FRI
folds, every opened FRI row and the final polynomial agree exactly.

The translations are triangular: the main translation is fixed, quotient
translations may depend on the main masks, and the randomizer translation
may depend on both. Each step is conditionally bijective on its uniform
mask space. Their composition preserves the full product distribution.

6. Efficient simulation

Sample the honest verifier coins and the query positions. Sample the
main values at Q union gQ union O(z) union O(gz) from their uniform
Frobenius-compatible evaluation spaces. These spaces are independent of
the witness because the main-mask evaluation map is onto.

Evaluate each constraint quotient at Q union O(z) from those main
values and the public preprocessing. Sample quotient-chunk observations
uniformly in the affine fibers of the corresponding Lagrange equations.
The translations in Section 4 show that these are their exact conditional
distributions. Sample the randomizer observations and public-padding
observations with their prescribed distributions.

The disclosed source values determine W at Q and O(z)\{z}. Conditional
on those values, the randomizer transport spans the complete kernel of
these evaluations. Thus W is uniform in the affine space of E-polynomials
of degree below 255 with those values. Its remaining dimension is

    255-(|Q|+deg(m_z)-1) = 256-|Q|-deg(m_z) >= 184.

Interpolate one such W, add a uniformly random kernel polynomial, and
compute its complete FRI fold sequence. Return its queried rows and
final polynomial together with the sampled direct observations. Every
step is finite-field linear algebra of polynomial size. All inverse
hints are determined by public challenges and query points.

This simulator establishes the distribution of the full algebraic
opening view from public (Y,I), including all quotient coordinates and
all FRI leaf values.

7. Exceptional coins and repeated use

Every proper subfield of E lies in F_(p^4). A uniform gamma therefore
fails the degree-eight basis condition with probability exactly p^-4.
The excluded z-domain probability is at most 8192/p^8. Taking a union
bound gives

    delta_alg <= p^-4 + 8192/p^8,
    -log2(delta_alg) approximately 123.95474.

Conditional on the complementary event, simulation is exact. The
unconditional total variation distance is at most delta_alg. S fresh
independent-coin authorizations have distance at most S*delta_alg by
successive conditional simulation. Classical adaptive request selection
is permitted in this algebraic honest-verifier experiment.

The implementation supplies seeded coins and hash-derived verifier
challenges. The joint generator hybrid and native adaptive quantum
compiler supply the additional terms for that experiment. Twenty uniform
base-field salt elements have entropy 20*log2(p), approximately 619.77
bits, in the independent-coin hybrid.

9. Causal algebraic sampling

The algebraic simulation can also sample its messages in verifier order.
At z, sample the main and quotient observations in the spaces described
above. Sample each R_j(z) uniformly in F(z), with the appropriate
Frobenius compatibility. Repeated opening points impose one shared value.
After gamma, consider the aggregate with no source query fixed yet.
The randomizer transport with |Q|=0 shows that W is uniform among
degree-below-255 E-polynomials with its determined values on O(z)\{z}.
This conditional law depends only on the disclosed observations.

Sample that complete W. Each subsequent beta_i determines its next fold
and eventually the final constant. After the source queries are sampled,
draw their main and quotient observations conditioned on the earlier
z-observations. The complete W is independent of their undisclosed
polynomials given those earlier observations, by the same surjection of
the randomizer masks.

For a queried base point q, write W_other(q) for the DEEP contribution
of the main, quotient and public preprocessing rows. The randomizer row
must satisfy

    sum_(j<16) gamma^j R_j(q)
      = sum_(j<16) gamma^j R_j(z)
        + (q-z) [W(q)-W_other(q)].

This is a surjective F-linear equation from F^16 to E. Choose the last
eight coordinates uniformly in F and solve uniquely for the first eight
using the gamma basis. It gives the uniform affine fiber at q.

These fibers are jointly compatible with the earlier R_j(z) and complete
W. Indeed the kernel of the coefficient map

    (u_0,...,u_15) -> sum_j gamma^j u_j

has F-dimension eight. Multiply polynomial vectors in this kernel by
m_z(X). Their evaluations cover the entire kernel independently at Q,
because |Q|+deg(m_z) <= 72 < 256. Thus this sampling law is the exact
conditional distribution of every disclosed randomizer row.

The construction samples the algebraic messages causally. simulation.txt
and compiler.txt establish the native commitments and continued quantum
experiment, including the complete buffered response groups.
