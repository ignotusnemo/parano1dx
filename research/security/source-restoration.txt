Public-coin knowledge and source restoration
9 November 2026

The fixed 4096-position profile has a polynomial-time prefix extractor
with round-by-round error

    kappa_public <= C(896,64)/C(4096,64) + N_f/p^8,
    p=2130706433.

The query term has negative binary logarithm 142.94109606984. The exact
finite first-order certificates and integer N_f are reproduced by
check_rs_certificate.py. This analysis uses 64 queries, the existing
blowup sixteen and the existing degree-eight challenge field.

1. Immutable words and finite interpolation certificates

Expose committed batches as immutable arrays. Keep every prover message
and verifier coin in its native order. Main words precede alpha.
Quotient and randomization words precede z. Opening claims precede gamma.
Each current FRI word precedes its folding challenge. The final constant
precedes the complete 64-position query move.

Use the finite first-order interpolation and counting results of Dao,
Kominers and Thaler, 2026/2056, Proposition C.2 and Lemma 5.5. They apply
to arbitrary prescribed evaluation domains. The characteristic conditions
are checked below for the actual prime, including when source columns
are encoded in a larger extension for interleaved list decoding.

For length n, dimension k, threshold A, multiplicity m, derivative degree
M and total jet degree B, put D=k-1. The derivative-weighted support has
monomials X^x Y_0^a Y_1^b satisfying b<=M, a+b<=B and
x+D*a+(D-1)*b<m*A. Its degree-t coefficient count and local rank bound are

    N_t = sum_(b=0)^min(t,M) max(m*A-D*t+b,0),
    R_t = sum_(s=0)^(m-1) 1_[s+(D-1)t<m*A]
          * max(0,min(m-s,min(t,s)-max(0,t-M)+1)).

Put C=sum_t N_t and R=n*sum_t R_t. Every certificate has C>R and

    (C-R)*H > R*B.

For a received curve of degree ell, this implies
C*(ell*H+1)>R*(ell*H+ell*B+1). A coefficient count therefore gives a
nonzero polynomial kernel vector of challenge degree at most ell*H.
Dividing its common univariate content makes its specialization nonzero
at every challenge. Substituting any degree-<k candidate with A agreements
then gives a polynomial of degree below m*A and at least m*A roots,
counting multiplicity. It vanishes identically. The support rank bounds
are Proposition C.2. This argument also explains why the line-to-affine
reduction is not used for powers batching.

The checked tuples are listed as (n,k,A,m,M,B,H).

    Source list:    (4096,257,896,256,157,888,405516956)
    Gamma and FRI:  (4096,256,896,192,126,672,177710)
    FRI:            (1024,64,224,64,42,227,53572)
    FRI:            (256,16,56,24,15,87,2838)
    FRI:            (64,4,14,4,2,18,390)

The Gamma tuple is used again for the first fold. All five finite certificates
satisfy p>max(D,M). The largest derivative degree is 157.
Every folding threshold equals ceil((7/32)*n).

2. List and curve bounds

Put lambda=(n-D)/(A-D), b=B*(2M+1) and h=H*(2M+1). The squarefree
first-order counting theorem gives the following conservative bounds:

    Lambda = ceil(4*D*lambda*B*M+2*B*M+B),
    E = ceil((2b-1)h+lambda*(h+b+4*D*b*h)+(n-D-1)b
        +(48*D^2*H+16*D)*lambda^2*B*M
        +8*D*(n-D-1)*lambda*B*M).

Lambda bounds every fixed-word list. A degree-ell received curve has
at most ell*E exceptional challenges. To see the latter specialization,
use challenge degree ell*H in Lemma 5.5. Its ordinary-resultant and
regular-family terms are bounded as in the proof of Theorem 5.2.
Each term is linear in the received-curve degree and this height, giving
the displayed ell*E envelope. For M=0, Remark 5.6 supplies the ordinary
case and the regular terms vanish.

At every other challenge, every candidate with A agreements admits
coefficient witnesses on its full agreement set. The counting proof
splits ordinary resultant solutions from regular differential solutions.
Taylor reconstruction uses p>D, and separability uses p>M. These are
exactly the characteristic guards checked for each tuple. Corollaries
6.4 and 6.6 transfer the list and curve statements to interleaved words
without a width factor.

Let Lambda_src be Lambda for the source-list tuple. This bound is
independent of the received word and field extension. Lists are decoded
by the first-order algorithms in Section 9 of the same paper. For an
interleaved source, encode all columns in an extension of the base field,
decode, and expand the coefficients in its fixed basis. The algorithm
is polynomial in the code length, field representation and certificate
parameters.

3. Restoration through the actual FRI folds

Each production domain is a multiplicative coset and each arity r is four.
Write t=x^r. Fourier decomposition of a current word v defines coefficient
words u_j(t) by

    v(x)=sum_(j=0)^(r-1) x^j u_j(x^r).

The actual native fold at beta is sum_j beta^j u_j(t). The field-matrix
checker compares this identity with the optimized two- and four-point
formulas in the streaming verifier. It checks every coordinate basis,
so the same linear identities hold over the extension field.

Suppose the next restored polynomial Q(t), of degree below k/r, agrees
with the folded word at the descendants of a good query set. Pull it
back to Q(X^r). On the current domain its full agreement set S contains
the good set and is a union of complete r-point orbits. Apply polynomial-
curve MCA to the words u_j(X^r), at degree r-1, using the current tuple.
Outside its exceptional set, it gives polynomials U_j(X) of degree below
k agreeing with those words on the full set S.

For a primitive r-th root omega, U_j(omega*X)-U_j(X) vanishes on S.
Its degree is below k, while |S|>=A>k. Therefore it is zero. Since r
is invertible, U_j(X)=Q_j(X^r) with degree Q_j<k/r. The polynomial
sum_j X^j Q_j(X^r) has degree below k and agrees with v on S. This
restores the previous word while retaining every original good query.
The full-set property supplies the orbit closure used in this step.

Starting with the transmitted final constant, repeat through arities
4,4,4,4. The code lengths and dimensions are (4096,256), (1024,64),
(256,16) and (64,4). The initial FRI coordinate is unshifted.
Pulling its polynomial back by the public source coset shift gives the
same degree bound and agreement set on the source domain.

4. Direct restoration of opening claims

Before gamma, each source column P_j and its advertised claim v_j at
z_j are fixed. Its DEEP word is

    d_j(q)=(P_j(q)-v_j)/(q-z_j).

Exclude z values where a public evaluation denominator vanishes. The
public domains and rotations cost at most 8192/p^8. The native batching
word is a powers combination of 268 such words, of curve degree 267.
Apply its finite Gamma certificate to the restored initial FRI polynomial.
It supplies degree-<256 polynomials R_j agreeing with every d_j on the
same full set, outside at most 267*E_gamma challenges.

Set P'_j(X)=(X-z_j)R_j(X)+v_j. It has degree at most 256, agrees with
the corresponding committed source on that set and takes the advertised
value at z_j. Two reconstructions of a main column, at z and g*z, agree
on more than 256 points. Hence they are identical and both claims are
its evaluations. The public preprocessing reconstruction likewise equals
its authenticated polynomial.

Each private source value on this set is in F_p. The coefficientwise
Frobenius difference of P'_j has degree at most 256 and vanishes at more
than 256 base-field points. It is zero, so P'_j has coefficients in F_p.
The reconstructed source tuples therefore belong to the lists of
dimension 257 fixed before their respective verifier coins.

5. AIR flags and exact field ledger

For every decoded main candidate, form all constraint remainders modulo
Z_H. An invalid trace has a nonzero remainder. Its alpha composition
is a nonzero polynomial of degree at most 61, giving at most
62*Lambda_src exceptional alpha values.

Outside this flag, pair each main candidate with every decoded quotient
candidate. The composed constraint polynomial minus the reconstructed
Z_H multiple is nonzero. The native outer equation evaluates this
polynomial at z. Its degree is at most 4608. This bound includes main
and quotient source degree 256, the degree-896 chunk selectors and
Z_H of degree 128. Thus all pairs cost at most
4608*Lambda_src^2 exceptional z values. Since Lambda_src^2>=8192,
the excluded public-domain points fit the additional Lambda_src^2.

The complete field numerator is

    N_f = 62*Lambda_src+4609*Lambda_src^2
          +267*E_gamma+sum_i (r_i-1)*E_i.

Every integer and characteristic guard is reproduced in
rs-certificate.json. The four E_i use their own finite tuples. All
constructor, field, commitment and verifier parameters are those of the
fixed implementation profile.

6. Query bound and round-by-round knowledge

Fix a complete transcript before its final query move. Its good-index
set contains exactly those source positions passing the complete native
query predicate. If its density exceeded a=7/32 and all flags above
were absent, backward folding and direct opening restoration would give
source candidates on that same good set. The outer equation would then
contradict the absence of the alpha and z flags unless a valid main
trace were in the decoded list. The prefix extractor enumerates that
list, checks the trace on the 128-point subgroup and returns its first
fourteen elements at logical row one as an authority preimage.

Consequently, on failed extraction and with all flags absent, at most
896 of the 4096 source positions pass. A uniform 64-element subset is
contained in that set with probability C(896,64)/C(4096,64).

The implemented query sampler uses one 512-bit response. Its low twelve
bits must be zero. The remaining 500 bits, reduced modulo M=C(4096,64),
select the subset by colexicographic rank. Every rank has at most
ceil(2^500/M) preimages. Its exact joint bad-response density is at most

    kappa_query = ceil(2^500/M)*C(896,64)/2^512.

Every field challenge is the radix-p expansion of a uniform 512-bit
integer reduced modulo p^8. A set of b extension values has density at
most ceil(2^512/p^8)*b/2^512. Thus

    kappa_field = ceil(2^512/p^8)*N_f/2^512,
    kappa = kappa_query+kappa_field.

The prefix extractor decodes the same finite source list, checks the
128-point trace with transition stride two and returns the secret at
logical row one. It uses the deterministic first-order decoder of
DKT26, Section 9. Its characteristic guard p>2*(B+1)^3 holds for B<=888.
The scalar source support and kernel-height guards are checked exactly
by check_rs_certificate.py. This is a polynomial-time extractor with
fixed code length 4096. Its full deterministic decoding cost is included
in the authority-preimage adversary in authorization.txt.
