Coherent response resource condition
9 November 2026

The Category 1 corollary uses logical bounded-fanin gates and logical
circuit depth. It fixes at most 2^64 classical authorization requests.
For each quantum batch s, with A_s gates, delta_s depth and k_sj newly
charged responses of type j, its resource condition is

    sum_j k_sj c_j <= A_s delta_s,
    c_j >= 2^20 gate-depth.

The conservative physical call count charges one gate per adversarial
call, so adversarial calls are bounded by G.
The batch price applies to the cheaper hash component, including
parallel execution, workspace reuse and coherent lookup costs. Input
and output projections retain this same declared floor.

The resource corollary applies to batches satisfying this minimum-cost
premise. The numerical evaluator records the complete success envelope
as a function of the price, including a unit-price row and sensitivity.

The concrete assessment in nist-resource-assessment.txt costs published
direct-call layouts and native fixed-prefix implementations in a stated
logical gate library. check_hash_circuits.py reproduces the native counts,
including output projections and a one-bit phase implementation. Its
data-only charge favors the attacker by omitting phase, cleanup and
fanout costs. The evaluated layouts support the selected reference price.
The batch condition above specifies the hypothesis for applying the
resource corollary to other circuit or quantum-memory implementations.

The ideal proof-layer calculation and the fixed authority-preimage,
derivation and private-generator games are separate inputs. The full
Category 1 conclusion applies when their combined advantage is below
the remaining half-success margin displayed in security-evaluation.json.

The NIST reference is AES-128 key search. Its quantum reference is
G*D=2^170 at D in {2^40,2^64,2^96}. Its classical reference is 2^143
gates. The resource theorem uses the complete bound including finite
simulation, recording and adaptive-update terms at those budgets.
